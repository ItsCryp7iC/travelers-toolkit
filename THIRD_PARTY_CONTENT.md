# Third-Party Content & Editorial Recommendations Notice

Traveler's Toolkit incorporates character build recommendations, stat priorities, weapon and artifact rankings, talent leveling recommendations, editorial gameplay notes, and theorycrafting credits provided by the **Genshin Impact Helper Team** community project ([Genshin-Impact-Helper-Team/genshin-builds](https://github.com/Genshin-Impact-Helper-Team/genshin-builds)).

---

## 1. Permission & Authorization

The Genshin Impact Helper Team has explicitly granted permission for Traveler's Toolkit to retrieve, normalize, cache, periodically synchronize, and display their community build recommendations with visible attribution.

In accordance with community best practices:
- Direct client-side GitHub API and raw content requests are avoided entirely.
- All recommendations are imported and normalized through offline tooling into static application datasets.
- Original editorial notes, tips, and external calculation links are credited directly to their respective authors and the Helper Team.
